Khetmti is the easiest way to complete the Holy Quran together with your family, friends and mosque. Create a khatma in seconds, share its code, let everyone reserve what they will read, and follow the progress live until the whole Quran is complete, in sha Allah.

WHO IS IT FOR?
• Families reading a khatma together in Ramadan and all year round
• Friends who want to compete in goodness
• Mosques, Quran circles and hifz classes
• Anyone dedicating a khatma to a loved one: "Khatma for my late grandfather"

CREATE A KHATMA IN SECONDS
• Write who the khatma is for
• Split it by surah (114 surahs) or by juz (30 juz)
• Set the maximum number of members
• Get a 5-digit join code to share with your loved ones

JOIN IN ONE TAP
• By code: enter the 5-digit code
• By name: search for the khatma and join right away

RESERVE YOUR PART, FINISH IT WITH A TAP
• Every juz or surah shows its state in words and colours: free, reserved for you, reserved by someone else, finished
• Reserve, release, mark as done, or undo
• Quick filters: all, free, mine, done

LIVE PROGRESS FOR EVERYONE
• The completion percentage updates instantly whenever a member reads
• Members page: who finished what, and who is reading now
• Activity log for every step of the khatma
• All your active and completed khatmas in one place

POINTS, LEVELS AND HEALTHY COMPETITION
• Earn points when you reserve and finish (a full juz = 500 points)
• Climb the levels and ranks: Student, Reader, Reciter, Mujawwid, Hafiz, Master, Faqih, Scholar, Imam
• Leaderboard by day, week or all time — worldwide or among friends
• Your statistics: how many times you read each surah and juz

YOUR FRIENDS ARE WITH YOU
• Find friends by name
• Send and accept friend requests
• Follow their levels and when they were last active

BUILT-IN MUSHAF
• The complete Quran text inside the app
• Open any surah or juz straight from your khatma
• Tap an ayah to save your stopping point; the app brings you back to it next time

FOUR LANGUAGES
• Arabic (main language), English, French and Spanish

PRIVACY FIRST
• Free, with no ads
• Delete your account and data from Settings at any time

May Allah accept from us and from you, and make this app an ongoing charity (sadaqa jariya).
